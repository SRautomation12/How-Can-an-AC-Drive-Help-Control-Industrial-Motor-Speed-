Does Your Motor Run at the Wrong Speed for the Job?
Some machines face changing workloads and don't always need full speed. Without proper control, motor operation is less flexible and may draw more energy than the process requires.
What Does an AC Drive Do?
An AC drive controls the speed of a compatible AC motor by adjusting the supply frequency and, where suitable, the voltage. Depending on the application, it allows smoother starts, speed adjustment and controlled stops.
How Do You Choose the Right Drive?
Check the motor rating, load type, speed range, operating environment, protection needs and control compatibility. Correct sizing and configuration make a real difference to reliability.
